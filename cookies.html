CozyMealCove Cookie Policy

Effective Date: September 8, 2026 • Clear Explanations of Web Cookies and Local Storage

1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage Tokens?

Cookies are compact text files placed onto your computer, tablet, or mobile device by websites that you visit. They are widely utilized across the internet to ensure website functionality, improve browsing efficiency, store user layout preferences, and furnish anonymous statistical reporting to site owners.

In addition to standard HTTP cookies, modern web browsers utilize "Local Storage" and "Session Storage" mechanisms. CozyMealCove utilizes these lightweight technologies to deliver a seamless, personalized reading and recipe planning experience without tracking sensitive personal details.

2. The Categories of Cookies Deployed on CozyMealCove

We categorize the cookies and storage tokens utilized on our digital platform into four clear functional groups:

  • Strictly Necessary & Functional Storage: These tokens are essential for core site navigation and interface stability. For example, our custom JavaScript uses browser LocalStorage (under the key cmc_theme) to remember your preferred visual mode—either our warm Linen light theme or our evening Hearth Charcoal dark mode—so you do not need to re-toggle the setting on each page load.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: We deploy Google Analytics (Measurement ID: G-0LY0HY7L01) to track anonymous aggregate metrics such as daily visitors, page load performance, and reader interaction with our culinary monographs. These cookies (such as _ga and _ga_*) do not store personal names or financial details; they collect anonymized IP data to help our editorial team identify which recipe categories resonate most with our community.
  • Interactive Tool Session Data: Inputs entered into our interactive Supper Feast Calculator (such as guest counts and recipe styles) are held purely in volatile client-side memory during your active session and are never transmitted to external third-party servers.
  • Security and Anti-Abuse Cookies: Used to protect our contact form and reservation endpoints against malicious automated bot submissions.

3. Managing, Disabling, and Deleting Cookies

You have the absolute right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. Most modern web browsers automatically accept cookies by default, but you can readily modify your browser settings to decline cookies or prompt you before accepting a cookie from websites you visit.

To manage cookies in common browsers, follow the links below:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Mozilla Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Enhanced Tracking Protection
  • Apple Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Please note that if you choose to reject or disable strictly necessary cookies and local storage tokens, certain functional features of CozyMealCove (such as automatic theme preservation) may not function as intended.

4. Analytics Data Governance & Transparency

Google Analytics (ID: G-0LY0HY7L01) telemetry gathered on our platform is evaluated solely on an aggregate, non-personally identifiable basis. We analyze metrics such as average reading time per monograph, popular device display resolutions, and browser performance to improve document formatting and mobile loading speed. We do not use advertising re-targeting cookies or third-party behavioral ad trackers.

5. Updates to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect modifications in the technologies we deploy or for operational and statutory compliance reasons. We encourage visitors to review this page periodically.
