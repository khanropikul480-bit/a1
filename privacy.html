Data Privacy & Governance

CozyMealCove Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 8, 2026 • Committed to Absolute Transparency and Digital Integrity

1. Introduction & Scope of Commitment

At CozyMealCove ("we," "our," "us," or "the Platform"), accessible via https://cozymealcove.com, we cherish the privacy and digital trust of our readers, supper club guests, home culinary enthusiasts, and newsletter subscribers. This comprehensive Privacy Policy governs the manner in which CozyMealCove collects, utilizes, safeguards, handles, processes, and discloses information gathered from users across our digital publications, recipe calculators, interactive dinner planners, and correspondence channels.

We recognize that your privacy is fundamental to a trusted online reading experience. Whether you are browsing our slow-braising monographs, utilizing our client-side Dinner Portion Calculator, reserving seats for a seasonal supper club feast, or subscribing to our Friday evening recipe dispatch, we ensure that your personal information is treated with highest levels of technical security, confidentiality, and statutory compliance.

By accessing or interacting with CozyMealCove, you acknowledge the data collection, processing, and handling practices described within this formal policy. If you do not agree with any terms outlined herein, we respectfully request that you discontinue using our digital platforms and subscription services.

2. Categories of Information We Collect

To deliver exceptional culinary content, maintain recipe functionality, and manage supper club reservations, we collect the following categories of information:

  • Personally Identifiable Information (PII): When you voluntarily register for our weekly recipe dispatch, submit a private dining inquiry, or request supper club reservations, we may collect your full name, email address, postal address, telephone contact number, and dietary restrictions or preference notes.
  • Technical & Analytical Device Telemetry: When you navigate our digital pages, our servers automatically record standard internet telemetry, including your Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser classification, operating system version, referring URL, pages viewed, time spent per recipe monograph, clickstream patterns, and interaction timestamps.
  • Interactive Tool Input Data: Information entered into our client-side Dinner Portion Calculator (such as guest counts and menu style selections) is processed locally within your browser session to generate instant portion recommendations and is never stored permanently on external servers.
  • Communication Records: Transcripts of emails, contact form submissions, and direct messages sent to our Asheville culinary team are retained for customer service records and recipe support history.
  • Preference Tokens: Lightweight local storage tokens that record your visual preference (such as enabling Hearth Charcoal dark mode) are stored on your local browser.

3. Lawful Bases and Purposes for Data Processing

CozyMealCove processes personal information exclusively under recognized lawful bases, including explicit user consent, legitimate operational interests, and compliance with applicable legal frameworks:

  • Delivering Editorial & Recipe Content: Transmitting our Friday Evening Feast Dispatches, seasonal menu updates, and requested recipe scaling advice to subscribed home cooks.
  • Managing Supper Club Bookings: Coordinating seating times, dietary modifications, and tableside hospitality arrangements for intimate dining sessions.
  • Platform Optimization & Performance Monitoring: Analyzing aggregate website traffic patterns to refine layout readability, optimize mobile browsing speed, and diagnose technical server issues.
  • Security Assurance & Spam Prevention: Protecting our digital infrastructure against fraudulent requests, denial-of-service disruptions, automated scraping, and unauthorized server intrusions.
  • Statutory and Legal Compliance: Complying with applicable commercial and tax accounting regulations for private culinary dining sessions.

4. Cookies, Tracking Technologies, and Google Analytics

CozyMealCove employs first-party and select third-party cookies, pixel tags, and local storage tokens to enhance user browsing experiences, preserve visual display preferences (such as our Hearth Dark Mode state), and analyze aggregate engagement.

We utilize Google Analytics (Tracking ID: G-0LY0HY7L01) to measure anonymous aggregate audience interaction, including session durations, geographic regions (at the city/country level), and popular culinary monographs. Google Analytics utilizes cookies stored on your browser to compile anonymous statistical metrics. To learn more about how Google processes analytical telemetry, or to install the official Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, please review Google's official privacy documentation.

Importantly, we do not deploy invasive cross-site advertising trackers or third-party data tracking networks. The analytical telemetry we collect serves exclusively to understand reader interest and improve our educational culinary publications.

5. Data Retention, Storage Security, and Minimization

We adhere to strict data minimization principles. We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the specific operational objectives for which it was originally collected, or as required by statutory record-keeping regulations. Newsletter subscription details are retained until you explicitly opt out or unsubscribe.

All electronic data transmissions are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / SSL) protocols. Our web servers, database repositories, and administrative tools are protected by multi-layered firewalls, strict role-based access permissions, and periodic vulnerability scanning. While no digital transmission over the public internet can be guaranteed 100% impervious to breach, we maintain industry-standard physical and electronic safeguards to defend your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.

6. Third-Party Disclosures and Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, lease, or barter your personal information to third-party data brokers, marketers, or advertisers under any circumstances. We may share limited data with trusted third-party service providers solely to facilitate our operational activities (such as managed cloud hosting providers, encrypted email delivery networks, and analytics measurement platforms). These service providers are bound by strict contractual confidentiality agreements and are prohibited from utilizing your information for independent commercial motives.

7. Global Data Rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, and CCPA/CPRA Compliance)

Depending on your geographic residency, you are entitled to exercise specific statutory data protection rights under regulations such as the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA):

  • Right of Access & Portability: Request confirmation of whether we process your personal data and obtain an export of your information in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Rectification: Request the prompt correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal records held within our databases.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten"): Request the complete deletion of your personal records, subject to legitimate legal retention mandates.
  • Right to Restrict or Object: Restrict certain data processing activities or object entirely to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: Receive equal service quality and pricing regardless of whether you exercise your privacy rights.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw your consent for email communications at any time by clicking the "Unsubscribe" link located at the bottom of every dispatch.

8. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

CozyMealCove is designed for home cooks, adults, and culinary enthusiasts. We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personal information from individuals under the age of 13 (or under 16 within applicable European jurisdictions). If we discover that personal information from a minor has been collected without verified parental consent, we will promptly purge such data from our servers.

9. Policy Revisions and Contact Information

We reserve the right to amend this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect technological advancements, kitchen updates, or statutory requirements. All revisions will be posted directly to this page with an updated "Effective Date." Continued use of our Platform following posted revisions constitutes acceptance of the amended policy.
