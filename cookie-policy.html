Cookie Policy

Last Modified: March 9, 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small alphanumeric files placed on your browsing device to retain preferences, streamline textile checkouts, and deliver anonymous website telemetry.

2. Categories of Cookies Deployed

  • Essential Cookies: Required for cart persistence, swatch selection security, and checkout processing.
  • Analytical & Performance Cookies: We utilize Google Analytics (G-0LY0HY7L01) to evaluate page engagement, bounce metrics, and browser compatibility.
  • Functional Setting Cookies: Retain your preferred measurement units (millimeters vs inches) during browsing sessions.

3. Modifying Cookie Settings

You can adjust, block, or delete cookies via your web browser preferences. Disabling essential cookies may impair the interactive custom ordering workflow.
