Our Lineage

The Architectural Bedrock of Haute Passementerie

Reclaiming the lost luxury of hand-loomed gold brocade trims and bullion fringe in Manhattan's historic SoHo arts district.

Reviving Century-Old Narrow Loom Mastery

In an era of mass-produced polyester ribbons and synthetic heat-stamped borders, Brocade Trimly was founded on an unyielding principle: true passementerie is an art of structural permanence.

Located at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, our atelier houses restored 19th-century jacquard narrow-fabric shuttle looms operating alongside modern laser warp sensors. Our core pillar is the preservation of authentic passementerie traditions: continuous gold-leaf threads, dense pure silk warp densities, and hand-twisted bullion fringes.

We work directly with couture fashion houses, period theatrical productions, and national museums to weave ribbons that do not merely adorn garments, but articulate their architectural presence in space.

⚜️

Pure Filament Sericulture

All warp threads are spun from Grade 6A continuous-filament Mulberry silk, providing profound tensile strength to withstand high-tension shuttle beating.

⚡

Real 24K Gold Metallurgy

Our metallic ribbons feature genuine 24K gold and 999 pure silver electroplating over micro-flattened copper filaments that never tarnish or corrode.

📐

Micro-Millimeter Customization

Every pattern point is drafted to the millimeter, allowing couturiers to calibrate trim stiffness, width, and bend radius to specific garment seams.

100% Manual Magnification QA
Craftsmanship

Uncompromising Quality Standards

Every single meter of ribbon woven in our studio undergoes two independent quality inspections under high-magnification lenses. We verify thread tension, selvedge straightness, and metallic ribbon overlap to guarantee zero skipped stitches.

Our bespoke client list includes award-winning costume designers, European royal conservators, and avant-garde runway creators who refuse to compromise on sartorial integrity.
