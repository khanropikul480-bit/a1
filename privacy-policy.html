Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Last Modified: March 9, 2026

1. Introduction & Overview

Brocade Trimly ("we", "our", or "us"), operating at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, recognizes the paramount importance of your personal confidentiality. This Privacy Policy details the policies and mechanisms governing how personal data is collected, stored, handled, and safeguarded when visiting https://brocadetrimly.com or placing bespoke passementerie orders.

2. Categories of Information Collected

In fulfilling bespoke textile orders, we process the following categories of data:

  • Client Identification & Delivery Records: Name, design studio address, telephone number, and electronic mail address provided during commission registration.
  • Custom Textile Measurement Specifications: Millimeter measurements, pattern repeat notes, and technical weaving requirements supplied for custom loom runs.
  • Payment & Billing Tokens: Encrypted transaction identifiers, billing addresses, and payment confirmation tokens processed through PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment vaults. We never store raw credit card numbers on our servers.
  • Digital Telemetry: Anonymized interaction logs, browser environments, and referral routes collected via analytical cookies (G-0LY0HY7L01).

3. Legal Bedrock for Data Processing

Our operational data practices are anchored upon legitimate bedrocks established under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and CalOPPA:

  • Contractual Execution: Weaving, cutting, and dispatching custom ordered metallic trims.
  • Legitimate Interests: Enhancing our weaving formulations, preventing ordering fraud, and optimizing shipping logistics.
  • Statutory Compliance: Fulfilling tax, customs import/export, and commercial accounting regulations.

4. Disclosure & Confidentiality

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data. Disclosures are strictly confined to vetted operational logistics partners (e.g. insured courier couriers and secure payment processors) operating under binding non-disclosure covenants.

5. Your Statutory Rights

You hold statutory rights to access, rectify, export, or request deletion of your account records at any time. To exercise these privileges, contact our Privacy Compliance Officer at atelier@brocadetrimly.com or call +1-888-777-5845.
